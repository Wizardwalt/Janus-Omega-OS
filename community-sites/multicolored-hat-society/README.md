# Multicolored Hat Society — Brought to You by Aria

Temporary staging location inside the Janus-Omega-OS ecosystem foundation repository.

This is a separate cybersecurity education and research website prototype, not a Janus OS subsystem and not the same thing as Aria's internal ten-domain knowledge taxonomy.

Future standalone repository:

`Wizardwalt/Multicolored-Hat-Society`

The Society has six communities: White Hat, Black Hat, Gray Hat, Blue Hat, Red Hat, and Green Hat. Learning and exercises must remain lawful and authorized: owned systems, explicit permission, or isolated labs. Harmful techniques may be studied conceptually for defense, but this site must not facilitate unauthorized compromise or real-world harm.

No production accounts, discussion service, moderation system, database, or deployment is included yet. Keep the prototype static and independently portable.
