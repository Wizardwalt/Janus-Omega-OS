Many perspectives.
Stronger security.

A community for cybersecurity education, research, responsible collaboration, and building safer systems through six complementary perspectives.

Six communities

White Hat

Ethical security practice, authorized testing, and responsible disclosure.

Black Hat

Understand adversary behavior and attacker thinking through safe, defensive study and isolated labs.

Gray Hat

Explore complex security questions, boundaries, ethics, and responsible research.

Blue Hat

Defense, detection, incident response, hardening, and resilience.

Red Hat

Adversary simulation and offensive-security concepts in explicitly authorized environments.

Green Hat

Beginner learning, fundamentals, mentorship, and skill development.

Our safety boundary

Hands-on exercises belong on systems you own, systems where you have explicit authorization, or isolated training labs. Studying malicious techniques for understanding and defense does not authorize using them against others.

Independent community

The Society will have its own membership, moderation, privacy, content, and governance. Aria integration will be optional and permission-scoped. The Society's six communities are distinct from Aria's internal knowledge-classification system.

Coming later

Research resources, lab guides, member discussions, mentorship, events, and responsible-disclosure workflows are planned. This page is currently a static prototype.
