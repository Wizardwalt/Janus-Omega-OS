# Janus Monolith (Stand) & Forge (Dock)

## 1. The Monolith (Display Stand)
- **Primary Function**: Pip-Boy storage and wireless charging.
- **Wireless Charging**: Integrated Qi-standard 15W transmitter coil.
- **Data Link**: Built-in USB-C 3.2 passthrough for data syncing while docked.
- **Construction**: Weighted black anodized aluminum base.

## 2. The Forge (Battery Dock)
- **Primary Function**: Magazine charging and health monitoring.
- **Capacity**: 4x Mjolnir Battery Magazines.
- **Charging**: 100W PD input with individual per-magazine smart-charging status.
- **Visuals**: Vertical charging slots with cyan "breathing" LED indicators for charge level.

## 3. Wireless Charging (Receiver)
- **Location**: Bottom-side of the Pip-Boy chassis.
- **Module**: High-efficiency ferrite-backed receiver coil.
- **Controller**: Integrated with the Mjolnir BMS for seamless switching between Kinetic, USB-C, and Wireless power.
