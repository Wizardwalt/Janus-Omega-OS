# Pandora Titan Housing Specification (CAD Guide)

## Material
- Weatherproof & Shock-Resistant: High-impact reinforced polymer with 6061 Aluminum internal skeleton.
- Weatherproofing: IP68 certified with silicone gaskets and sealed port covers.
- Shock Resistance: MIL-STD-810H compliant rubberized bumpers and internal vibration dampening.

## Design Elements
- **Aesthetic**: Heavy "Retro-Future" industrial look inspired by the 'Vault-Tec' Pip-Boy design, featuring a weathered olive-drab bulky casing, brass exposed hardware, and tactical mechanical toggles.
- **Forearm Mount**: Ergonomic curved wrist-lock with breathable heavy-duty nylon straps.
- **Display**: 7.0" Ultra-Widescreen Capacitive Touchscreen (1280x480) - integrated into the bulky industrial frame.
- **Controls**: 
  - Dual Large Analog-Style Rotary Knobs (Volume/Frequency)
  - 5-Way Navigation Joystick
  - Chorded Key Array (Mechanical Switches)
- **Connectivity & Storage**: 
  - Dual SIM Slots (Waterproof sealed)
  - 1x MicroSD Slot (Waterproof sealed)
  - Internal M.2 Access hatch (Shock-resistant)
  - High-gain internal MIMO antenna array (LTE/5G/Wi-Fi/BT)
  - Built-in microphone and waterproof speaker for voice calls
- **Panic Button**: Guarded physical toggle switch (Red).
