# Multicolored Hat Society — Brought to You by Aria

**Status:** Foundation charter; independent repository and website not yet created.

## Purpose

The Multicolored Hat Society is an independent cybersecurity education, research, and collaboration community. It brings together six perspectives for understanding security, learning technical skills, improving defenses, and responsibly researching vulnerabilities.

It is a separate product and website from Janus Community. It is not a Janus OS module browser, and membership does not grant access to Janus tools or systems.

## The six communities

### White Hat
Ethical security testing, system protection, security standards, hardening, and responsible disclosure.

### Black Hat
Study attacker behavior, malicious techniques, and threat tradecraft to understand how attacks work and improve detection and defense. This track is for analysis, threat modeling, controlled demonstrations, and defensive understanding—not operationalizing harm against real people or third-party systems.

### Gray Hat
Vulnerability research, authorization boundaries, responsible disclosure, reproducible findings, and the ethics of working near unclear permission boundaries. Practical testing requires explicit authorization or an isolated lab.

### Blue Hat
Defensive engineering, monitoring, detection, incident response, forensics, recovery, resilience, and security operations.

### Red Hat
Authorized adversary simulation, penetration testing, validation of defenses, and controlled security assessments against explicitly approved targets.

### Green Hat
Experimentation, programming, creative security engineering, tool development, learning projects, and innovation in controlled environments.

## Shared operating rules

- Practical exercises use owned systems, explicit authorization, or isolated labs.
- Scope, target ownership, and permission must be established before testing.
- Responsible disclosure and remediation are encouraged.
- Do not facilitate unauthorized access, credential theft, malware deployment, destructive activity, evasion of accountability, or harm to third parties.
- Do not publish personal data, secrets, access tokens, or unredacted sensitive evidence.
- Label proof-of-concept work, simulations, lab-only code, and production-tested tools accurately.
- Preserve references, hashes, timestamps, and reproduction steps for research claims.
- Provide defensive context and remediation guidance with vulnerability education.

## Relationship to Aria

Aria may support education, evidence organization, research summaries, and defensive analysis through permissioned integrations. Her internal knowledge taxonomy is a separate ten-hat system; the Society remains a six-community organization. The two must not be conflated.

The Society does not inherit Aria's permissions, Janus OS execution rights, or OmniVault access. Any integration is explicit, scoped, revocable, and audited.

Use the attribution **“Multicolored Hat Society — Brought to You by Aria”** where accurate. Do not imply an active Aria integration when one is not connected.

## Governance foundation

Before public launch, establish human administrators, trained moderators, a research disclosure policy, rules for lab content, incident reporting, appeals, data retention, and a transparent safety review process.

This charter is not a substitute for a complete legal review, platform terms, privacy policy, or operational moderation system.
