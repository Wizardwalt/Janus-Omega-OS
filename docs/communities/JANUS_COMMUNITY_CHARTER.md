# Janus Community — Powered by Aria

**Status:** Foundation charter; independent repository and website not yet created.

## Purpose

Janus Community is an independent public technology community for people who build, repair, understand, and use electronics and computing systems. It is intended to make practical knowledge easier to share and preserve across computers, phones, operating systems, electronics, device servicing, software, and everyday technical problem-solving.

It is not merely a forum category inside Janus OS or the Multicolored Hat Society.

## Core areas

- Electronics, components, circuit design, embedded systems, and hardware projects.
- Computers, operating systems, Linux, Android, networking, and infrastructure.
- Phone and device repair, diagnostics, maintenance, and recovery.
- Software development, automation, tools, and open-source projects.
- Practical tutorials, build logs, troubleshooting, documentation, and knowledge exchange.
- Maker projects, cyberdecks, portable computing, and the Pandora hardware lineage.
- Community projects, collaboration, resource sharing, and responsible technical research.

## Community principles

1. Practical usefulness and clear technical explanations.
2. Respectful participation and constructive disagreement.
3. Accurate claims: label experiments, owner-tested results, independent verification, failures, and unknowns.
4. Preserve provenance and credit original authors.
5. Respect device ownership, privacy, safety, and applicable law.
6. No harassment, doxxing, credential theft, unauthorized access, or malicious activity.
7. No fabricated test results or claims that a project works when it has not been tested.
8. Keep the community useful without requiring Janus OS, Aria, or any particular AI provider.

## Relationship to Aria

Use the attribution **“Janus Community — Powered by Aria”** where accurate. Aria may help with search, summarization, documentation, and learning only when an actual, permissioned integration is available. Do not imply that Aria is online or processing community data when she is not.

Community data belongs to the community and its contributors under the eventual published terms and privacy policy. Aria does not receive blanket access to member accounts, private messages, or unpublished submissions.

## Governance foundation

Before public launch, establish:
- named human administrators and moderators;
- a transparent moderation and appeals process;
- contribution and attribution rules;
- privacy, retention, and deletion policies;
- reporting and abuse-handling procedures;
- an accessibility and conduct standard;
- clear distinctions between community advice and professional repair/safety guidance.

This charter does not itself create legal terms, privacy promises, or a deployed moderation system. Those require review before launch.
